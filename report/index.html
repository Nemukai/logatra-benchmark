How well do speech APIs actually hear India?

Koe Benchmark measures transcription accuracy, speed, and cost across Indic languages, Hinglish code-mixing, and real-world telephony audio. Public datasets give the baseline; a fresh held-out set collected after every provider's training cutoff gives the honest number.

Method, honestly

Two-layer design

Public datasets (FLEURS, MUCS 2021, Gramvaani) are almost certainly in providers' training data; they are reported as the contaminated baseline. The held-out layer is Creative-Commons YouTube audio published January–June 2026, segmented and reference-checked after collection. It cannot have been trained on.

Silver references

Held-out references come from multi-model consensus (medoid under a script-agnostic distance, cluster-agreement gate). Sarvam, Gemini, and Whisper contributed hypotheses, so their held-out scores are upper bounds; Voxtral and gpt-audio are fully independent entrants. 36 of 132 chunks failed consensus and await human adjudication.

Metrics

Indic-aware normalization before scoring: NFC, Indic punctuation and digit mapping, whitespace collapse. Script-agnostic WER additionally romanizes Devanagari (IAST, schwa-deleted) so "स्टार्ट" and "start" match. Latency is wall-clock per API call from this network; treat comparatively, not absolutely.

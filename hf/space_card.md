Interactive leaderboard and per-case explorer for the **Logatra Benchmark**, an
independent speech-to-text benchmark for Indian languages and Hinglish built by
[Nemukai](https://nemukai.com), the team behind [Logatra](https://logatra.com).

- Data, audio and raw provider outputs:
  [CooledNik/logatra-benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/CooledNik/logatra-benchmark)
- Code and methodology (MIT): [github.com/Nemukai/logatra-benchmark](https://github.com/Nemukai/logatra-benchmark)
- Also live at [nemukai.com/labs/logatra-benchmark](https://nemukai.com/labs/logatra-benchmark)
